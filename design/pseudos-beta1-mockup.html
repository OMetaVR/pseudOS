Try it

  • Tap an icon to open it. Guest apps are mocks; system apps work.
  • Tap the handle at the bottom to go home. Hold it for the app switcher. Keys: H and S.
  • Drag a card up in the switcher to quit that app.
  • Long-press an icon for its menu, including Edit Home Screen.
  • Open a 5th app to hit the running limit (4).
  • The chips under the clock jump to updates, running apps and signing.
  • Settings has Signing with Refresh now, and Appearance.

Look

Every install gets its own contour wallpaper from a seed; the amber mark is its summit. The accent marks attention only: badges, running dots, progress, the armed handle.
